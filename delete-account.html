Sudoku Park - Data Deletion

Sudoku Park is a Sudoku puzzle game developed by Nishanth Devagiri.

Sudoku Park does not require an account or sign-in. Your gameplay progress is stored locally on your device.

What Data Is Stored?

Sudoku Park stores the following gameplay information on your device:

  • Saved and completed puzzles
  • Daily Challenge progress and streaks
  • Experience points (XP)
  • Achievements and statistics
  • Game settings and preferences

This information is used to save your progress and maintain gameplay features.

How to Delete Your Gameplay Data

You can delete Sudoku Park's locally stored gameplay information using your Android device settings.

  1. Open your Android device's Settings.
  2. Go to Apps.
  3. Select Sudoku Park.
  4. Open Storage or Storage & cache.
  5. Select Clear Storage or Clear Data.
  6. Confirm the action.
Important: Clearing app storage permanently removes locally saved Sudoku Park progress, including completed puzzles, XP, achievements, and preferences.

The exact menu names may vary depending on your Android device.

Uninstalling Sudoku Park generally removes locally stored app data. Device backups may retain copies depending on your backup settings.

Account Deletion

Sudoku Park does not currently create user accounts.

Therefore, users of the current version do not have a Sudoku Park account that needs to be deleted.

If you have a question about data associated with an earlier version, please contact us using the email address below.

Advertising Data

Sudoku Park uses Google AdMob to display advertisements.

Advertising-related information may be processed separately by Google and its advertising partners.

Clearing Sudoku Park's local storage does not necessarily delete information held by those third parties.

For more information, visit Google's Privacy Policy .
