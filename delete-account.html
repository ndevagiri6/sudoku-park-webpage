Delete Your Sudoku Park Account

If you have created a Sudoku Park account by signing in, you can permanently delete your account directly from the Sudoku Park app.

How to delete your account

  1. Open Sudoku Park.
  2. Open the More/Settings section.
  3. Go to Account & Sync.
  4. Select Delete Account.
  5. Type DELETE when asked for confirmation.
  6. Confirm the account deletion.

You must be signed in to the account you want to delete.

What is deleted

Deleting your Sudoku Park account removes the associated cloud account and synced data, including:

  • Account and sign-in associations
  • Cloud synchronization data
  • Synced game progression and XP
  • Synced achievements
  • Synced statistics and completion history
  • Daily Challenge completion history stored in the cloud
  • Synced preferences
  • Active Sudoku Park account sessions

Data stored on your device

Deleting your cloud account does not automatically delete gameplay data stored locally on your device. You can remove local progress using the reset option available in Sudoku Park or by clearing/removing the app's local data from your device.

Subscriptions

Important: Deleting your Sudoku Park account does not cancel an active Google Play or Apple App Store subscription.

If you have an active Sudoku Park Pro subscription, manage or cancel it separately through the store where you purchased it.

After deletion

After your account is deleted, Sudoku Park signs you out and you can continue using the app as a guest.
