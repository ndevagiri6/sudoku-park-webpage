Sudoku Park - Data and Account Deletion

Sudoku Park is a Sudoku puzzle game developed by Nishanth Devagiri.

The current version of Sudoku Park does not require an account or sign-in. Gameplay progress is stored locally on your device.

Do I Need to Delete an Account?

No. The current version of Sudoku Park does not offer account registration or sign-in. You can play Sudoku Park without creating an account or providing account registration information.

If you have only used the current version, there is no Sudoku Park account to delete.

Deleting Gameplay Data From Your Device

Sudoku Park stores gameplay information locally, including:

  • Saved and completed puzzles
  • Daily Challenge progress and streaks
  • Experience points (XP) and achievements
  • Gameplay statistics
  • Game settings and preferences

You can remove locally stored gameplay data using any available reset options in Sudoku Park or by clearing the app's storage through your device settings.

Uninstalling Sudoku Park generally removes its locally stored data. However, device backup services may retain copies depending on your device settings and backup provider.

Deleting local gameplay data does not necessarily remove information separately processed by third-party advertising providers.

Historical Cloud Accounts

Earlier development or testing versions of Sudoku Park may have supported optional accounts and cloud synchronization.

Account creation and cloud synchronization are disabled in the current release.

If you previously created a Sudoku Park account, your historical account information and synchronized gameplay data may still be stored using Cloudflare infrastructure.

You may request deletion of your historical account and associated cloud data using the instructions below.

How to Request Historical Account Deletion

  1. Send an email to ndevagiri6@gmail.com .
  2. Use the subject Sudoku Park Account Deletion Request.
  3. Mention that you want to delete your historical Sudoku Park account and associated cloud data.
  4. Provide the email address associated with your previous Sudoku Park account, if known.
Security: We may request additional verification to confirm that you are authorized to delete the historical account. Never send your password or authentication codes by email.

Once the request has been verified, we will process deletion of the associated account and cloud data, subject to any applicable legal, security, or backup-retention requirements.

What Historical Account Deletion Covers

Depending on the information previously stored, deletion may include:

  • Sudoku Park account records
  • Associated sign-in identities
  • Stored account sessions
  • Cloud-synchronized puzzle progress
  • Cloud-synchronized XP and achievements
  • Cloud-synchronized gameplay statistics
  • Daily Challenge completion records
  • Cloud-synchronized preferences

Data that must be retained for applicable legal or security reasons, or data contained in backups, may be subject to separate retention periods.

What Happens After Deletion?

Once your historical cloud account is deleted, its associated cloud records will no longer be available for synchronization.

Any gameplay progress stored locally on your device is managed separately.

You can continue playing Sudoku Park normally without an account.

Subscriptions and Purchases

Sudoku Park does not currently offer paid subscriptions or premium purchases.
