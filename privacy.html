Sudoku Park Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 22, 2026

Sudoku Park is a Sudoku puzzle game developed by Nishanth Devagiri. This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use Sudoku Park.

No Account Required

Sudoku Park can be played without creating an account or signing in. Account registration, Google and Apple sign-in, and cloud synchronization are not available in the current version of the app.

You can access the available puzzles and gameplay features without providing your name, email address, or other account registration information.

Information Stored on Your Device

Sudoku Park stores gameplay information locally on your device. This may include:

  • Puzzle progress and completed puzzles
  • Daily Challenge progress and streaks
  • Experience points (XP) and achievements
  • Gameplay statistics and records
  • Game settings and preferences
  • Saved and unfinished puzzles

This information is used to maintain your progress and provide gameplay features. The current version does not upload new gameplay progress to Sudoku Park's cloud synchronization service.

Local gameplay information generally remains on your device until it is removed through available app controls, clearing the app's storage, or uninstalling the app. Device backup services may separately retain app data according to your device settings and the backup provider's policies.

Cloud Synchronization and Historical Data

Cloud synchronization and account creation are disabled in the current release of Sudoku Park.

Previous development or testing versions may have allowed account-based synchronization using Cloudflare Workers and Cloudflare D1.

Information previously synchronized may include puzzle progress, achievements, Daily Challenge history, statistics, and preferences.

Disabling synchronization does not automatically delete previously stored cloud information. Existing historical account data may remain until it is deleted through an authorized deletion request or applicable retention processes.

For questions or requests concerning historical cloud information, please contact us using the email address provided below.

Advertising

Sudoku Park displays advertisements provided through Google AdMob. These may include interstitial advertisements and rewarded advertisements.

Google and its advertising partners may collect or process information such as device identifiers, advertising identifiers, approximate location derived from network information, app interactions, diagnostic information, and advertising activity.

This information may be used to deliver, measure, secure, and, where permitted, personalize advertisements.

Where required, Sudoku Park uses Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) to request applicable privacy and advertising consent choices.

You may be able to review or update applicable privacy choices through the app's privacy settings, where available.

For more information about how Google processes information, visit: Google Privacy Policy .

Subscriptions and Purchases

Paid subscriptions and premium purchases are not available in the current version of Sudoku Park.

The current version does not request payment information for premium gameplay features.

Data Retention and Deletion

Gameplay progress and preferences are stored locally on your device and are not currently synchronized to Sudoku Park's cloud service.

You can remove local app information by using available reset controls, clearing the app's storage, or uninstalling Sudoku Park.

Clearing local data does not necessarily remove information separately processed by advertising providers or retained in device backups.

Although account creation is unavailable in the current release, users who created accounts through earlier versions may request deletion of their historical cloud account and associated data.

Historical account deletion requests can be submitted through our Data Deletion Page or by emailing the privacy contact below.

We may need to verify that the requester is authorized to delete the relevant historical account information.

Historical cloud records and any associated backups are handled according to applicable retention and deletion procedures. Specific retention periods can be requested using the contact information below.

Data Sharing and Sale

Sudoku Park does not sell personal information directly to data brokers.

Advertising-related information may be collected, processed, or shared by Google AdMob and its advertising partners in accordance with their applicable policies and user consent choices.

Historical cloud information may be processed by Cloudflare where required to maintain or delete previously stored account data.

Data Security

Sudoku Park uses local device storage for gameplay information in the current release.

Requests involving historical cloud account information use authenticated and encrypted connections where applicable.

Reasonable technical measures are used to protect information handled by Sudoku Park. However, no method of electronic storage or transmission is completely secure.

Children's Privacy

Sudoku Park is a general-audience puzzle game. We do not knowingly collect personal information directly from children in violation of applicable law.

Third-party advertising services may process information subject to applicable privacy requirements and the app's configured advertising settings.

Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when Sudoku Park introduces new features or changes its information-handling practices.

Any updates will be published on this page, and the "Last updated" date will be revised.
