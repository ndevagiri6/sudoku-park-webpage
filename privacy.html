Sudoku Park Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 22, 2026

Sudoku Park is a Sudoku puzzle game developed by Nishanth Devagiri. This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use the app.

Information We Collect and Store

Sudoku Park does not require users to create an account or sign in.

Gameplay information is stored locally on your device. This includes:

  • Puzzle progress and saved games
  • Completed puzzles
  • Daily Challenge progress and streaks
  • Experience points (XP) and achievements
  • Gameplay statistics
  • Game settings and preferences

This information is used to maintain your progress and provide gameplay features.

Sudoku Park does not upload gameplay progress to its own servers during normal use.

Advertising

Sudoku Park uses Google AdMob to display advertisements, including interstitial and rewarded advertisements.

Google and its advertising partners may automatically collect or process certain information, including:

  • IP address and approximate location derived from network information
  • Device identifiers, including advertising IDs where available
  • Advertisement views and interactions
  • App usage information related to advertising
  • Diagnostic and performance information

This information may be used for advertising, measurement, fraud prevention, security, and personalized advertising where permitted.

For more information, please review Google's Privacy Policy .

Advertising Consent and Privacy Choices

Sudoku Park uses Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) to manage applicable advertising consent and privacy choices.

Where required, users may be shown a consent message before certain advertisements are requested.

Applicable privacy choices may be reviewed or updated through the privacy settings available in the app.

Data Sharing

Sudoku Park does not sell locally stored gameplay progress or upload it to Sudoku Park's own servers during normal play.

Advertising-related information may be collected, processed, or shared by Google AdMob and its advertising partners according to their privacy policies and applicable consent choices.

Data Retention and Deletion

Gameplay information is stored locally on your device and generally remains until it is removed.

You can delete locally stored gameplay information by clearing Sudoku Park's app storage through your device settings.

Uninstalling the app generally removes its locally stored data, although device backup services may retain copies depending on your device settings.

Information separately processed by Google and its advertising partners is subject to their applicable retention and deletion policies.

For additional instructions, visit our Data Deletion Page .

Data Security

Sudoku Park stores gameplay information in the app's local device storage.

We take reasonable measures to protect information handled by the app. However, no method of electronic storage or transmission is completely secure.

Children's Privacy

Sudoku Park is a general-audience puzzle game.

We do not knowingly collect personal information directly from children in violation of applicable law.

Third-party advertising services may process information according to applicable privacy requirements and advertising settings.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when the app's data-handling practices change.

Updates will be published on this page with a revised date.
