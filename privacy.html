Sudoku Park Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 7, 2026

Sudoku Park is a Sudoku puzzle game that can be played without creating an account. This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use Sudoku Park.

Playing Without an Account

You can use Sudoku Park without signing in. When you play as a guest, gameplay information such as puzzle progress, statistics, achievements, preferences, and other game progress is stored locally on your device.

Optional Account and Cloud Sync

You may optionally sign in using a supported sign-in provider, such as Google or Apple. Signing in allows supported game progress and preferences to be backed up and synchronized between devices.

Cloud synchronization is provided using Cloudflare infrastructure, including Cloudflare Workers and D1. Synced information may include progression, achievements, Daily Challenge completion history, statistics, and selected preferences.

Subscriptions and Purchases

Sudoku Park offers optional premium features. Subscription and purchase information is managed using RevenueCat and the applicable app store, such as Google Play or the Apple App Store.

Payment details are handled by the applicable app store. Sudoku Park does not directly collect or store your credit or debit card information.

Advertising

Free users may be shown advertisements provided through Google AdMob, including rewarded and interstitial advertisements. Advertising providers may process device, advertising, and usage information as necessary to provide, measure, secure, and personalize advertising where permitted.

Where required, Sudoku Park will request applicable advertising or privacy choices before serving certain advertisements.

Account Deletion

Signed-in users can request deletion of their Sudoku Park account from within the app's Settings. Account deletion removes the associated Sudoku Park cloud account and synced cloud data.

Information stored locally on your device may remain after cloud account deletion unless you separately reset the app's local progress or remove the app's local data.

Deleting your Sudoku Park account does not automatically cancel an active Google Play or Apple App Store subscription. Subscriptions must be managed through the applicable app store.

Data Sharing and Sale

Sudoku Park does not sell your personal information. Information may be processed by service providers used to operate features such as sign-in, cloud synchronization, subscriptions, and advertising.

Data Security

Reasonable technical measures are used to protect information handled by Sudoku Park. However, no method of electronic storage or transmission is completely secure.

Children's Privacy

Sudoku Park is a general-audience puzzle game. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children in violation of applicable law.

Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated as Sudoku Park changes. Updates will be published on this page and the "Last updated" date will be revised.
